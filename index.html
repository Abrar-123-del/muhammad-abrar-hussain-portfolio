01About

I’m a Master’s researcher in Computer Engineering, working at the intersection of deep learning, computer vision, and Mixed Reality. Most of my research asks the same question in different forms: given a camera pointed at part of a moving body, what is the smallest, most reliable signal a model needs to recognize intent?

That has meant comparing convolutional and transformer architectures on static hand gestures, building a two-stream temporal model for dynamic egocentric gestures in VR, and designing FootSense — a body-worn 360° camera system for recognizing foot-based input when your hands are busy holding a headset controller. I integrate what works into Unity-based MR prototypes.

TSMTFN: Two-Stream Temporal Shift Module Network

A two-stream network for efficient egocentric dynamic hand-gesture recognition in VR, using temporal shift modules in place of heavier 3D convolutions.

gesture recognition · VR · efficiency

DenseNet vs. ViT for Static Hand Gesture Recognition

A comparative study on HaGRID, weighing convolutional inductive bias against transformer attention for static gesture classification.

DenseNet · Vision Transformer · HaGRID

FootSense: Body-Worn 360° Camera for Foot Input

A waist-worn 360° camera, aimed down at the feet, that recognizes foot-based gestures for Mixed Reality — freeing the hands for other input.

360° vision · foot interaction · MR
Submitted · VRST (poster)

From Ten to Five: Confusion-Guided Vocabulary Reduction

Uses confusion patterns between gesture classes to shrink a ten-gesture vocabulary to five for more reliable single-view foot-gesture recognition.

vocabulary reduction · single-view
